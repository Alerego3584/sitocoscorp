How I got here

I started by photographing people in costume. Conventions, original collabs, long nights grading a character until the armor looked like metal instead of foam.

Brands started asking for the same control in offices and on stage. The kit did not change much. The brief did. I still run both, because the craft is the same: light, timing, and a file that matches what we agreed on set.

Progress for me is quieter than a reel. Better light. Faster setups. Cleaner grades. More jobs where I stay on the file from the first frame to delivery.

What I bring

  • Direction Posing, blocking, and a clear look before we roll.
  • Lighting Studio, mixed venue light, and small kits that still hold a mood.
  • Motion Short video next to stills when the job needs both.
  • Post Color and delivery handled here, not sent out.
